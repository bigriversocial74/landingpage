# Microgifter

Microgifter is a mobile-friendly social-gifting, merchant CRM, loyalty, campaign,
claim, redemption, and automated-commerce platform.

## Product purpose

Local customers want better gift choices. Independent businesses want easier digital
commerce without more hardware, fragmented software, or technical maintenance.
Microgifter connects them.

## Core capabilities

- Product, service, experience, and creative-work gifting
- Merchant CRM and customer lifecycle records
- Campaigns, offers, rewards, referrals, and contests
- Customer messaging connected to transaction history
- Purchase, inbox, sent, claim, and redemption lifecycle tracking
- Recurring gifting, workplace rewards, and agent-assisted commerce
- Future-demand and Pre Sale Revenue concepts
