# Roger Huston — Music and Visual-Art Portfolio

**Public site:** https://rogerhuston.com/  
**Creative owner:** David Evans  
**Record and publishing company:** Ganjafesto Records, LLC  
**Location:** Phoenix, Arizona

## Artist identity

Roger Huston is David Evans's music and visual-art identity. Dave presents himself as
a songwriter, producer, singer, rhythm guitarist, and graphic designer with more than
25 years of original music and artwork experience.

Dave describes the Roger Huston sound as **Space Reggae**: futuristic music rooted in
reggae rhythm and sound, without fitting cleanly into reggae, hip-hop, or pop.

## Music-production workflow

- Sources licensed material from an online sample library.
- Exports selected sounds into Reaper.
- Layers, edits, arranges, and reshapes the material.
- Produces, mixes, and writes lyrics as the song develops.
- Sometimes uses MIDI tools for bass or guitar parts.
- Occasionally works with his friend Bo on lead guitar.
- Uses an online mastering service for final mastering.
- Performs as a singer and rhythm guitarist in live-band settings.

## Direct-to-fan commerce

- Music links for Spotify, Apple Music, Tidal, YouTube Music, and related profiles.
- Print-on-demand apparel, mugs, posters, and visual-art products.
- Printful-described fulfillment for standard merchandise.
- Personalized 12-inch vinyl records.
- Customer-selected playlists of roughly 20 minutes per side.
- Custom record title, optional back-cover artwork, and optional personal note.
- Affiliate-assisted custom-record configuration and commission workflow.

## Creative and business ecosystem

Ganjafesto Records, LLC is Dave's record and publishing company. The Roger Huston site
also identifies Stonefellow as another name under which Dave writes and records.

## Live Chat With Roger and VP3

Live Chat With Roger is an artist knowledge-agent concept using a live-avatar service.
The agent can be supplied with curated data such as transcripts, products, blog posts,
lyrics, events, commentary, and location information.

VP3 is described as the identity and knowledge-management layer:

1. Register an agent domain.
2. Upload or transcribe the agent's knowledge.
3. Create the live avatar.
4. Connect the avatar identifier to the VP3 domain or another website.

## Creative-authorship policies

- Roger Huston music is described as human-made.
- AI is not used for writing, performance, or production, apart from online mastering.
- AI-generated images may be used as preliminary concepts or visual references.
