# Homestead - Household Food Operating System

**Repository:** https://github.com/bigriversocial74/foodfarm
**Product owner:** David Evans
**Primary stack:** PHP, MySQL, HTML, CSS, JavaScript

Homestead coordinates the full household food lifecycle. It began as a frontier-style
cooking and sustainable domestic agriculture concept: bread, tortillas, pizza dough,
vegetables, microgreens, canning, dehydrating, bulk ingredients, starter kits, recipes,
food inventory, garden monitoring, and grow-light schedules.

## Connected areas

- Household and Family Members
- Roles, permissions, responsibilities, skills, and participation
- Pantry, prepared food, leftovers, and the food lifecycle ledger
- Recipes, meal plans, serving calculations, and shopping
- Storage locations and environmental targets
- Garden zones, planting plans, readings, grow lights, and harvests
- Canning, dehydrating, preservation batches, and preserved-food output
- Starter kits and mixed fulfillment
- Recurring tasks and household work planning
- Forecasting, seasonal planning, and self-sufficiency intelligence
- Costs, waste, savings, supplier comparisons, and recipe economics
- Nutrition data quality and meal-plan assessments
- Alerts, notifications, digests, and shared household calendar
