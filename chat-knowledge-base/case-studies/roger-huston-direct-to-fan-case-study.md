# Case Study — Roger Huston Direct-to-Fan Music Commerce

## The challenge

Many independent artist websites function as simple promotional pages. Music links,
merchandise, visual identity, personalized products, fulfillment, affiliate service,
publishing, and fan conversation are commonly separated.

## The system

Dave developed Roger Huston as a connected creative and commerce environment:

- Space Reggae music and artist positioning
- Streaming and playlist links
- Original graphic artwork
- Print-on-demand apparel, mugs, and posters
- Personalized vinyl records
- Custom playlists, record names, artwork, and notes
- Affiliate-assisted order configuration
- Ganjafesto Records publishing and commerce structure
- Live Chat With Roger artist agent
- VP3 agent-domain and knowledge-management concept

## Dave's role

Dave serves as songwriter, producer, singer, rhythm guitarist, graphic designer,
product designer, ecommerce builder, workflow designer, and agent-experience strategist.

## Business logic

Print-on-demand products reduce inventory exposure. Personalized vinyl introduces a
higher-touch custom product. Affiliate assistance creates a service and commission layer.
The knowledge agent gives the artist identity a conversational interface.

## Result

Roger Huston demonstrates how an independent creator can connect a music catalog to
visual art, physical products, personalization, publishing, affiliate participation,
and conversational media instead of relying on a conventional portfolio alone.
