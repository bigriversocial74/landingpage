# Case Study — Microgifter

## The challenge

Customers who want to shop locally often have fewer convenient digital gifting choices than
they have with national brands. Independent merchants may offer gift certificates, loyalty,
promotions, customer messaging, and redemption through separate tools—or not offer them at all.
Additional hardware, setup, and technical maintenance can make adoption harder.

## The product strategy

Microgifter was designed as one mobile-first environment connecting:

- Social gifting and mobile-friendly gift certificates
- Local products, services, experiences, and creative work
- Merchant CRM and customer lifecycle records
- Campaigns, rewards, referrals, contests, and offers
- Customer messaging linked to transaction and claim history
- Claim, redemption, and ownership-state tracking
- Recurring programs, workplace rewards, and agent-assisted commerce

## Differentiation

Microgifter is not positioned as only a traditional gift-certificate utility. It treats gifting
as a measurable customer and commerce lifecycle. Gifts, rewards, referrals, recognition
programs, and sponsored purchases can represent committed future demand and create ongoing
merchant relationships.
