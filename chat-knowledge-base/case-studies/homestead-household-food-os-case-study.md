# Case Study - Homestead Household Food Operating System

## Challenge

Household food work is fragmented across pantry lists, recipes, shopping notes,
garden schedules, preservation records, family responsibilities, budgets, alerts,
and calendars.

## Solution

Homestead uses a connected food lifecycle and household model. Family identity,
inventory, recipes, prepared food, meal plans, garden activity, harvests,
preservation, shopping, tasks, forecasts, costs, nutrition planning, and
notifications share household-scoped records.

## Dave's role

Product concept, V1 requirements, data architecture, family roles and privacy,
phased delivery, interface direction, QA, certification, and development coordination.

## Result

A multi-phase PHP/MySQL application with household isolation, transactional workflows,
provenance, idempotency, lifecycle controls, health diagnostics, and broad testing.

As reviewed July 26, 2026, merged repository work extends through Phase 10.
Phase 11 is open and is not represented as deployed production functionality.
